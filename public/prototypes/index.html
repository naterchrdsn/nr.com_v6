Five homepage concepts

Exploratory nebula homepages for Nate Richardson. Each is a standalone page with a Canvas2D sky (no WebGL needed), the same five real links, a motion pause control and a finished still for reduced motion. Use Previous / Next inside any concept to step through them in order.

  1. 01

    1 · Observatory

    Name upper-left, destinations along the lower edge with fine leader lines into an open sky. Click or tap the sky to send light echoes through irregular dust shells.

    observatory.html
  2. 02

    2 · Constellation

    Five labelled star destinations joined by thin lines that light up on hover and focus. Opens with a “first light” newborn star illuminating the clouds, with a replay control.

    constellation.html
  3. 03 NATE
    RICHARDSON

    3 · Typography

    A huge NATE RICHARDSON filled with nebula, with slow luminous dust beams sweeping behind and across the letters. Small intro and simple links underneath.

    typography.html
  4. 04

    4 · Spectrum

    Free-floating identity above a spectral strip of labelled destination lines. A real range slider blends the sky live between natural colour and a narrowband palette.

    spectrum.html
  5. 05

    5 · Horizon

    A cinematic sky over a dark ridge with a small observatory. A comet with dust and blue ion tails, occasional meteors, and a button to send a comet on demand. Text sits on the dark ground.

    horizon.html
